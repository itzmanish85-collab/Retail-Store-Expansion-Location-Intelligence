# Business Insights

## Headline Findings
- The highest-scoring expansion market is **Noida** with an opportunity score of **73.26**.
- The top five recommended markets are: Noida, Mysuru, Nashik, Thane, Raipur.
- **West** is the highest-revenue existing region in this synthetic portfolio.
- The highest-revenue existing store is **S059 (Mumbai)**.
- The expansion model balances demand, growth, income, competition, and estimated rent rather than selecting markets using revenue alone.

## Recommended Decision
Prioritize the top-ranked candidate markets for feasibility studies. Before committing capital, validate the model with actual lease quotes, competitor mapping, footfall studies, local demographics, and store-level unit economics.

## Limitations
This is a synthetic portfolio project. The scores demonstrate an analytics methodology and should not be interpreted as real-world market forecasts.
